Overview

The EAS8 Congress, taking place in Lisbon from 8 to 10 April 2027, will bring together leading international experts and the orthodontic community for three days dedicated to scientific excellence, clinical advancement, and innovation in aligner orthodontics.

The programme combines scientific lectures, hands-on workshops, interactive discussions, and dedicated sessions exploring the latest developments in orthodontic techniques and digital technologies.
