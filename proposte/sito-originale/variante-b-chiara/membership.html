What membership gives you

Our membership offers access to events, educational materials, and a vibrant professional network.

  • Congress benefits

    Registration benefits for the EAS8 Congress. Early bird registration is reserved exclusively for members.

  • Video lectures

    Complimentary access to video lectures from past Congresses and Meetings.

  • Partner offers

    Specific benefit offers from partner companies.

Membership options

How to become a member

Three steps, all online. Already registered on the EAS website? Log in and start from step 2.

  1. Create your EAS account

    Use Login at the top of any page to sign in or create your account.

  2. Join or renew

    On the Membership page, select the option that fits you: regular member or student / post-doctoral student.

  3. Check your status

    Your membership status is always available in your personal member area, once you are logged in.

Join before you register for EAS8

Early bird registration for the Lisbon Congress is reserved for EAS Members. Non-members can register from 29 December 2026.
