How to become an EAS Member

First, you need to create an EAS account by going to the Homepage and scrolling down to SIGN IN or UPDATE. Once completed, proceed to the next step to JOIN or RENEW.

See the three steps on the Membership page

EAS membership provides registration benefits for the EAS8 Congress, complimentary access to video lectures from past Congresses and Meetings, and specific benefit offers from partner companies.

EAS Membership, valid from January 1 to December 31 of each year, is available for regular members and students/post-doctoral students.

If you are already registered, ensure you are logged in. To proceed, go to the Membership page and select the appropriate one from the available options.

You can find all information on the status of your membership in your personal member area. To access it, you must be logged in.
