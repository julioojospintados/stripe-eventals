Become part of EAS to gain access to exclusive resources, connect with experts and stay at the forefront of aligner orthodontics.

Become a member How to join

What membership gives you

Access to events, educational materials and a vibrant professional network.

  • Congress registration benefitsMembers get the EAS8 early bird registration, reserved exclusively for EAS members.
  • Video lecturesComplimentary access to video lectures from past EAS Congresses and Meetings.
  • Partner offersSpecific benefit offers from EAS partner companies.

Membership options

Membership runs for the calendar year, from 1 January to 31 December.

Regular member

For professionals in the orthodontic field. Includes the EAS8 member registration benefits, video lectures and partner offers.

Join as a regular member

Student / post-doctoral

For students and post-doctoral students. Membership plus a validated student status gives access to the exclusive student fees at the Congress.

Join as a student

Fees are shown when you choose your membership.

How to become a member

1. Create your account

Use “Login” at the top of any page to create your EAS account. If you already have one, simply log in.

2. Choose your membership

On this page, select the option that fits you: regular member or student / post-doctoral.

3. Find it in your member area

The status of your membership is always available in your personal member area once you are logged in.
