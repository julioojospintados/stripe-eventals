About us

The European Aligner Society advances the science and practice of aligner orthodontics through education, research and collaboration.

Mission and activities

Our mission

At the European Aligner Society (EAS), our mission is to advance the science and practice of aligner orthodontics. We aim to provide a platform for education, research and collaboration among professionals in the orthodontic field.

Who we are

EAS is dedicated to promoting excellence in aligner orthodontics through comprehensive educational programmes, innovative research and community engagement. We bring together clinicians and researchers.

What we do

We organise annual congresses, webinars and workshops, providing opportunities for continuous learning and professional development. Our publications and newsletters keep members informed about the latest advancements and trends in aligner orthodontics.

Our focus

Education

Offering cutting-edge courses and webinars.

Research

Supporting and disseminating the latest studies.

Community

Building a network of top orthodontic professionals.
