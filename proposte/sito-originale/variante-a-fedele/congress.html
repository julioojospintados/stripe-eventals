EAS8 Congress Lisbon

8–10 April 2027

Mastering Clinical Challenges in Aligner Orthodontics. From Clinical Limitations to Predictable Outcomes.

Get your ticket Plan your stay

Three days of science and clinical practice

The EAS8 Congress, taking place in Lisbon from 8 to 10 April 2027, will bring together leading international experts and the orthodontic community for three days dedicated to scientific excellence, clinical advancement, and innovation in aligner orthodontics.

The programme combines scientific lectures, hands-on workshops, interactive discussions, and dedicated sessions exploring the latest developments in orthodontic techniques and digital technologies.

Programme at a glance

  • Three days8, 9 and 10 April 2027.
  • Parallel scientific sessionsIn two auditoriums.
  • Hands-on workshopsAlongside the main programme.
  • Interactive discussionsAnd dedicated sessions on techniques and digital technologies.

The detailed preliminary agenda has not been published yet. It will appear here as soon as it is available.
