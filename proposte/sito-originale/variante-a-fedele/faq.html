How to become an EAS member

First, create an EAS account with “Login” at the top of the page. Once completed, choose your membership to join or renew.

EAS membership provides registration benefits for the EAS8 Congress, complimentary access to video lectures from past Congresses and Meetings, and specific benefit offers from partner companies.

EAS membership, valid from 1 January to 31 December of each year, is available for regular members and for students and post-doctoral students.

Make sure you are logged in. Then go to the Membership page and select the appropriate option.

The status of your membership is in your personal member area. To access it, you must be logged in.
