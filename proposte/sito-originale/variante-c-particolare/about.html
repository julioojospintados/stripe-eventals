About us

Who we are, what we do and the people who lead the European Aligner Society.

Our mission

At the European Aligner Society (EAS), our mission is to advance the science and practice of aligner orthodontics. We aim to provide a platform for education, research, and collaboration among professionals in the Orthodontic field.

Who we are

EAS is dedicated to promoting excellence in aligner orthodontics through comprehensive educational programs, innovative research, and community engagement. We bring together clinicians and researchers.

What we do

We organize annual congresses, webinars, and workshops, providing opportunities for continuous learning and professional development. Our publications and newsletters keep members informed about the latest advancements and trends in aligner orthodontics.

Our focus

Education
Offering cutting-edge courses and webinars
Research
Supporting and disseminating the latest studies
Community
Building a network of top orthodontic professionals
