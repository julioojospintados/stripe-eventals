Frequently asked questions

Membership and EAS8 registration. Not answered here? Contact the EAS Office.

Membership

First, you need to create an EAS account with “Log in” at the top of the page. Once completed, go to the Membership page to join or renew.

EAS membership provides registration benefits for the EAS8 Congress, complimentary access to video lectures from past Congresses and Meetings, and specific benefit offers from partner companies.

EAS Membership, valid from 1 January to 31 December of each year, is available for regular members and students/post-doctoral students.

If you are already registered, make sure you are logged in. Then go to the Membership page and select the appropriate option.

You can find all information on the status of your membership in your personal member area. To access it, you must be logged in.

EAS8 Congress
