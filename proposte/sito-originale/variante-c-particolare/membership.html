How to become a member

Questions about your status? Write to office@eas-aligners.com or read the FAQ.

  1. Create your EAS account

    Use “Log in” at the top of the page to sign in or create your account.

  2. Choose your membership

    Once logged in, go to the Membership page and select the option that fits you: regular, or student and post-doctoral.

  3. Check your status

    Your personal member area shows the status of your membership. You must be logged in to see it.
