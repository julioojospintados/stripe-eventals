
    How to Become an EAS MemberHow do I become an EAS member?First, you need to create an EAS account by going to Homepage and scrolling down to SIGN IN or UPDATE. Once completed, proceed to the next step to JOIN or RENEW.What are the benefits of EAS Membership?EAS membership provides registration benefits for the EAS8 Congress, complimentary access to video lectures from past Congresses and Meetings, and specific benefit offers from partner companies.What are the available membership options?EAS Membership, valid from January 1 to December 31 of each year, is available for regular members and students/post-doctoral students.I am already registered on the EAS website, what is the next step?If you are already registered, ensure you are logged in. To proceed, go to the Membership page and select the appropriate one from the available options.Where can I find information about my Membership?You can find all information on the status of your membership in your personal member area. To access it, you must be logged in.EAS8 CongressHow do I register for the next EAS Congress?First, you need to create your account on the website by clicking “Sign In” at the top right corner of the page. You can then select “EAS8” from the main menu, choose your ticket and proceed with the registration.I’m not an EAS Member, can I register for the next Congress?The early bird registration is reserved exclusively for EAS Members. Non-members will be able to register starting from December 29, 2026. Take advantage of the benefits we offer to our members — join us today!I’m a student, can I attend the Congress?Absolutely! To qualify for the exclusive student fees, students must become EAS Members and validate their student status. For more information, please contact us at office@eas-aligner.com. What happens if I need to cancel my registration?All cancellations must be made in writing to the EAS Office: office@eas-aligners.comPlease note that cancellation fees apply as follows: A 50% cancellation fee will be charged for cancellations received between October 28, 2026 and March 1, 2027 (inclusive). No refund will be made for cancellations received after March 1, 2027.
