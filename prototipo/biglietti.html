      The member price: how to get it

      Members

      The yearly membership costs and is active right after payment. It gives the member price on the full pass, the early bird price (members only) and the member area with videos of past congresses. Workshops, companion and exhibitor tickets cost the same for everyone.

      The member price applies only to the member’s own ticket. If you also buy for other people, they pay the public price.

      Buying for others

      In an order the first ticket is in the buyer’s name, and only that one gets the member price. The other tickets carry their own names and cost the public price. The member price applies once per event (one full pass), even across orders.

      Names are final: no ticket can be passed on to someone else, at any price. A member friend who wants the member price buys with their own account.

      To buy at the member price, log in first.
